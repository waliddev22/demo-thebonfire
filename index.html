Good To Know

Questions we get a lot

Do you serve alcohol?
Yes — we run a full bar. And every mixed drink on the menu also comes in an alcohol-free version by default, with spirit pairings on hand for anyone who wants them. So whether you're drinking or not, nobody's stuck choosing between soda water and nothing.
Can you accommodate allergies and dietary restrictions?
This one matters to us. The menu includes clearly marked gluten-free, plant-based, and allergy-friendly options throughout — not just a token substitution here and there. If you're managing a specific allergy (nuts, shellfish, dairy, etc.), let your server know when you order; our kitchen can walk through ingredients and adjust where possible. We believe it's easy to be accommodating, and we'd rather you ask twice than worry once.
Do you take reservations?
Yes, for parties of any size — walk-ins are always welcome too, but a reservation guarantees your table, especially on trivia nights (Brooklyn 99, Marvel, and more, roughly every other week). For larger groups or private bookings, email us at bonfirehalifax@gmail.com and we'll sort out timing and space.
Do you host private events?
We do — birthdays, work socials, fundraisers, you name it. Reach out with your headcount, date, and what you're picturing, and we'll let you know what's possible in the space and put together a plan. The earlier you reach out, the more flexible we can be on timing.
Do you offer catering?
Yes — comfort food off the grill travels well. Whether it's an office lunch or a full event, get in touch with your group size and date and we'll put a menu together, including gluten-free, plant-based, and allergy-friendly options. Head to the Catering page for the full rundown, or email us directly to start the conversation.
Can I order online for pickup or delivery?
Yep. You can order directly through the Order Now button for pickup, or find us on Uber Eats for delivery. Heads up that third-party delivery pricing is set by the platform, not us — dining in or picking up directly always gets you our actual menu prices.
Is there parking?
There's street parking along Robie St and the surrounding blocks near Cunard — metered during the day, free in the evenings in most spots, but signage rules the day, so give it a glance. If you're coming for a bigger group or an event, message us ahead of time and we can point you to the closest reliable option.
